A protocol, not a party

An early draft of an open framework built on human dignity, nonviolence and accountable power. Work in progress.

Four principles

1

Human dignity comes before any ideology.

2

If an idea needs violence to win, it has failed.

3

No power should be too big to correct peacefully.

4

Technology should expand human agency, not obedience.

We do not seek to create a perfect society. We seek to create a society capable of continuously becoming better without requiring violence, obedience, or ideological uniformity.

-Human Protocol
